# B1 — Discovery Questionnaire (Internal Playbook)

**Document Code:** B1
**Document Name:** Discovery Meeting Playbook — 4-Phase Consultant Script
**Audience:** Venkat + any sales consultant who runs the first prospect meeting
**Total meeting duration:** 2h 15min (default); compressible to 1h 30min for smaller prospects
---

## What This Is

This is the **internal script** Venkat (or any consultant at Chronova) uses to run the **first structured meeting with a prospect** — the conversation that determines whether the engagement is worth pursuing and lays the foundation for the Statement of Work.

The playbook is organised as **4 phases with time-boxes**, mirroring how a senior HR consultant at a Big-4 firm (Deloitte / KPMG / PwC / EY) would structure a discovery interview: open context first, drill into structured intake next, then technical fit, then commercial viability. Each phase has its own conversation goals, sample questions, listen-for cues, and notes fields.

The playbook also includes:
- A **red flags** section (5-8 patterns that should make the consultant cautious or walk away)
- A **good signs** section (5-8 patterns that indicate a healthy client)
- A **follow-up email template** to send within 24 hours of the meeting
- A **scoring rubric** the consultant uses to grade the meeting (used by Venkat to compare prospects)

## Who Uses It

- **Primary user:** Venkat Narasimha (Founder, Chronova)
- **Secondary user:** Any sales / pre-sales consultant brought on to scale the sales process
- **NOT shared with:** The prospect, the prospect's employees, or any external party

## When To Use It

Use this playbook for the **first meeting** with any prospect who has expressed serious interest in Frappe HR (typically: they have responded to a marketing email, attended a webinar, or been referred by an existing client).

**Do NOT use for:**
- First cold outreach (use the landing-page CTA flow)
- Demo-only meetings (use the demo script)
- Existing clients (use the QBR / success playbook)

## 2. Meeting Structure — 4 Phases

### Phase 1 — Open Context (~30 minutes)

**Goal:** Understand the prospect's world before pitching anything. Build rapport. Identify the "why now" trigger.

**Tone:** Conversational, curious, generous with silence. **Listen 70%, talk 30%.**

#### 2.1.1 Org Overview (10 min)

| # | Question | Listen for | Notes |
|---|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | "Tell me about [Institution name] — what makes it distinctive in [city/region]?" | How the prospect positions themselves (academics, placements, research, hospital specialties) | Capture verbatim quotes — useful for marketing later |
| 2 | "Who are your biggest competitors in this region, and what do they do better than you?" | Strategic awareness; possibly reveals concerns (e.g., "they have a hospital; we don't") | |
| 3 | "What's your institution's growth trajectory over the next 3-5 years? New programmes? New campus? New hospital beds?" | Capital projects, expansion plans — affects scope and timeline | If expanding, time is on our side (they need a system) |
| 4 | "How is the institution governed? Who sits on the Board / Management Committee?" | Decision-making complexity; chairman's role | Helps us map the political landscape |

#### 2.1.2 Current Pain (10 min)

| # | Question | Listen for | Notes |
|---|----------|-----------|-------|
| 5 | "What's the single biggest HR pain point that's keeping you up at night?" | The "real" reason they're meeting us (not the marketing message) | Often different from the formal RFP |
| 6 | "Walk me through a recent HR incident that went wrong. What broke? Who was affected?" | Concrete example; shows operational maturity | Note: payroll error, leave dispute, attendance fraud, exam-duty conflict, regulatory audit finding |
| 7 | "What HR tasks are you still doing on paper or Excel?" | Volume of workarounds — these are the wins to highlight in SOW | |
| 8 | "How long does it take to process a leave application end-to-end today?" | If > 2 days, we have a strong case | |
| 9 | "How do employees find out their leave balance today?" | If "by asking HR", we have a strong case for self-service portal | |
| 10 | "Last time a regulatory audit happened (PF / ESI / Income Tax / NAAC), what did you struggle with?" | Compliance gaps | This is where Frappe HR's reporting shines |

#### 2.1.3 Decision Criteria (5 min)

| # | Question | Listen for | Notes |
|---|----------|-----------|-------|
| 11 | "What does a successful HR system look like for you 12 months from now?" | Their success criteria — should map to Frappe HR capabilities | |
| 12 | "What's the one thing a new system MUST do, and the one thing it MUST NOT break?" | Non-negotiables | Often "must do: leave balance visibility" / "must not break: PF calculation" |
| 13 | "Are you evaluating other vendors? If so, who else is on your shortlist?" | Competitive intel | Darwinbox, Keka, greytHR, SAP, custom Excel — each has different positioning |

#### 2.1.4 Stakeholders (5 min)

| # | Question | Listen for | Notes |
|---|----------|-----------|-------|
| 14 | "Besides you, who else needs to be in the room for this decision?" | Decision-making body (often Chairman + Principal + CFO + IT) | |
| 15 | "If we agree to move forward, who would be the day-to-day project owner from your side?" | Their HR Lead's capacity and authority | If no one, red flag |
| 16 | "Who would resist this change internally, and why?" | Politics — vital to map | Common: "older staff resistant to digital" / "union concerned about attendance tracking" |

**Phase 1 deliverable:** A clear understanding of the prospect's **why now**, **what hurts**, and **who decides**. Capture all answers in the consultant's notebook (or a structured note-taking doc).

### Phase 2 — Structured Intake (~60 minutes)

**Goal:** Walk through [[A1-org-profile]], [[A6-leave-policy]], and [[A7-approval-matrix]] live. Capture every answer. Don't preach — just ask the question, write down the answer, and move on.

**Tone:** Methodical, efficient. **Listen 50%, talk 50% (mostly clarifying questions).**

#### 2.2.1 A1 — Org Profile (15 min)

Open the A1 form in a shared Google Doc (or screen-share the CSV template). Walk through §1 → §5, asking each field:

| Section | Key questions | Common gaps |
|---------|----------------|-------------|
| **1.1 Identity** | "What's the legal name as on PAN?" "Display name on your website?" "Type — engineering, medical, university, polytechnic?" | Prospect may not have GSTIN if turnover is low |
| **1.2 Locations** | "How many campuses?" "Are any in different states?" | Multi-state = different Shops & Establishments Acts |
| **1.3 Accreditations** | "Which approvals do you hold?" "Latest NAAC grade?" "NIRF rank?" | If "we're accredited but not sure for how long" — flag as risk |
| **2.1 Employees** | "How many teaching staff? Non-teaching? Medical? Housekeeping outsourced?" | Most prospects over- or under-count; verify against payroll |
| **2.2 Payroll** | "In-house, outsourced, or hybrid?" "Monthly payroll cycle day?" "Statutory deductions handled?" | Outsourced payroll = additional integration |
| **2.3 IT** | "Current HR software?" "Biometric devices and how many?" "Email provider?" | If no biometric, attendance is a different problem |
| **2.4 Decision-makers** | "Who's the Chairman? Principal? HR Head? CFO? IT Head?" | **Critical:** get the Chairman's name — they are the budget signatory |
| **2.5 Constraints** | "Target go-live date?" "Why this date?" "Hard deadline?" | If "we want it yesterday", be honest about timing |

#### 2.2.2 A6 — Leave Policy (20 min)

Walk through the 9 leave types, capturing each attribute:

> "For each leave type, I'll ask: accrual rate, accrual period, carry-forward limit, max consecutive days, encashment, medical cert threshold, who it applies to, who approves, and notice period. We have defaults from the Indian labour law perspective — let's see where you're different."

| Leave type | Critical question | Watch out for |
|------------|-------------------|---------------|
| **CL** | "What's the cap on consecutive CL days?" | Some colleges allow 5; most cap at 3 |
| **SL** | "When is a medical certificate required — from Day 1 or after 2 days?" | Hospital staff may have stricter rules |
| **EL** | "How many days per year? Carry-forward cap? Encashment cap?" | **High litigation risk** — be precise |
| **Maternity** | "26 weeks for first 2 children, 12 for 3rd — is that your policy?" | Most private colleges follow; state-level variations exist |
| **Paternity** | "How many days? Statutory? Voluntary?" | No statutory mandate; common range 7-15 |
| **Comp Off** | "When does it expire? 30, 60, 90 days?" | Drives use-it-or-lose-it behaviour |
| **LWP** | "Max consecutive LWP? 90 days?" | Affects gratuity and PF — flag this |
| **OD** | "Approved by HOD or Principal? TA/DA admissible?" | Critical for teaching staff (FDPs, conferences) |
| **Special** | "Bereavement, marriage, study, paternity — covered under one or separate Leave Types?" | Many colleges keep "Special" as a single catch-all |

#### 2.2.3 A7 — Approval Matrix (25 min)

Walk through the 10 document types. The most common refinement: the prospect's chain is simpler (3 levels) or stricter (5 levels) than the defaults.

| Document type | Critical question | Watch out for |
|---------------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Leave** | "L1 = reporting officer, L2 = HOD, L3 = Principal + HR?" | Some colleges want L1 = HOD directly (skip reporting officer) |
| **OD** | "Does it need Principal sign-off or just HOD?" | Depends on whether TA/DA is paid |
| **Shift Change** | "Auto-approve same-day swaps?" | Yes for hospitals, rarely for colleges |
| **Comp Off** | "Verified by whom? Biometric check required?" | Strict vs lenient |
| **Attendance Reg.** | "Cap on regularizations per month?" | Some colleges cap at 3/month |
| **Tour** | "Threshold for Chairman sign-off? ₹50K? ₹1L?" | Varies wildly |
| **Loan** | "Max loan = how many months' gross salary?" | Common: 3 months |
| **Reimbursement** | "Auto-approve recurring claims up to how much?" | Common: ₹3,000/month |
| **Medical** | "Tie-up with CGHS / ESIC / private hospital chain?" | Affects integration |
| **Resignation** | "Notice period? 30 / 60 / 90 days?" | Teaching staff often have 90 days |

**Phase 2 deliverable:** Three completed intake forms. Consultant **saves the Google Doc** to the prospect's folder in the CRM within 4 hours of the meeting end.

### Phase 3 — Technical Fit (~30 minutes)

**Goal:** Verify Frappe HR is technically viable for the prospect's environment. Surface integration risks. Set expectations on data migration complexity.

**Tone:** Technical, specific. **Listen 30%, talk 70% (the consultant is now explaining what's possible).**

#### 2.3.1 IT Landscape

| # | Question | Listen for | Our positioning |
|---|----------|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | "What's your current IT stack? Servers, cloud, OS?" | On-premise vs cloud vs hybrid | If on-premise legacy, hosted Frappe Cloud saves IT time |
| 2 | "Do you have a dedicated IT team for HR systems?" | If < 2, expect capacity gap | We can supplement with Chronova managed service |
| 3 | "What does your email infrastructure look like? Google Workspace / Microsoft 365?" | Determines email-notification delivery | Frappe HR integrates with both via SMTP / OAuth |
| 4 | "Do you have Active Directory or any SSO?" | Determines user provisioning | Frappe HR supports LDAP / SAML / OAuth |
| 5 | "Internet uptime — last 30 days?" | If < 95%, hosted is risky | Recommend on-premise for low-uptime prospects |

#### 2.3.2 Biometric / Attendance

| # | Question | Listen for | Our positioning |
|---|----------|-----------|------------------|
| 6 | "What biometric / attendance devices do you use? Brand? Model? Count?" | eSSL, Mantra, ZKTeco, Realtime are common | Frappe HR integrates via Employee Checkin with device push / SFTP |
| 7 | "How do the devices talk to your current system? Direct API / SFTP / Excel?" | Real-time API = best; Excel manual = ongoing pain | We can automate via custom script |
| 8 | "How many employees per device? Any geolocation or RFID requirements?" | 50-100 per device is normal; 200+ = wait time | Affects Shift Type design |
| 9 | "Is the attendance data 100% reliable today, or are there manual corrections?" | Manual correction volume tells us about device deployment | If > 5% manual, device redeployment may be needed |

#### 2.3.3 Current Software & Data

| # | Question | Listen for | Our positioning |
|---|----------|-----------|------------------|
| 10 | "What HR / payroll software are you on today? Version?" | SAP, Oracle, Darwinbox, Keka, greytHR, Tally, Excel | Migration is a known cost; we have templates |
| 11 | "How long on current software? When was the last migration?" | If < 2 years, expect migration fatigue | Be honest: "this will be the 3rd system in 3 years" |
| 12 | "Can you export employee, leave, attendance, payroll data? In what format?" | CSV / Excel = easy; PDF = hard | Verify with a sample export during the meeting |
| 13 | "How many years of historical data do you need to migrate?" | 1-3 years is normal; 5+ = scope creep | 5+ years = data archival, not active migration |
| 14 | "Any custom integrations we should know about? Tally? SAP? Biometric middleware?" | Each integration is a work-package | List and price each |

#### 2.3.4 Integration Constraints

| # | Question | Listen for | Our positioning |
|---|----------|-----------|------------------|
| 15 | "Any systems that must NOT change? Accounting, SIS, LMS?" | Constraints we work around | Frappe HR is the system of record for HR; we integrate with others |
| 16 | "Who manages your current biometric? Same vendor for how long?" | Vendor lock-in risk | If expiring, we can recommend alternatives |
| 17 | "Do you use any payroll bank-file integrations? Which bank?" | SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis are common | We support standard bank file formats |
| 18 | "Any audit / regulatory requirements we're missing? PF, ESI, PT, TDS, LWF?" | Compliance checklist | Frappe HR is India-compliant out of the box; verify state-specific PT |

**Phase 3 deliverable:** A clear picture of the technical landscape and a list of integration risks to address in the SOW.

---

### Phase 4 — Commercial (~15 minutes)

**Goal:** Establish budget range, decision timeline, and success criteria. **Not a hard sell** — a healthy commercial conversation.

**Tone:** Transparent, non-pushy. **Listen 60%, talk 40%.**

| # | Question | Listen for | Our positioning |
|---|----------|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | "Have you allocated budget for this? For which financial year?" | FY 2026-27 or 2027-28? | We typically invoice 50% on signing + 50% on go-live |
| 2 | "Are you comparing this to a license model (Darwinbox / Keka) or a SaaS model (Frappe Cloud)?" | Affects TCO conversation | Frappe Cloud is per-user-per-month; on-premise is annual license |
| 3 | "What is the per-employee-per-month budget you have in mind?" | Benchmark: ₹150-300/user/month for SaaS, ₹50K-1L for on-premise license | If wildly off, we discuss alternatives |
| 4 | "When do you need to make a decision? When do you want to go live?" | If < 60 days for go-live, scope is constrained | Be honest about what 60 days can deliver |
| 5 | "What's your procurement process? Single-vendor / multi-vendor / tender?" | Determines how we respond | Tender = more paperwork; we can support it |
| 6 | "Who needs to sign the contract? Is there a legal team that reviews?" | Map the signatory chain | Send a draft contract within 5 working days |
| 7 | "What would make this a no-go for you?" | Real objections, not polite ones | Address each honestly; don't over-promise |
| 8 | "If we agree to move forward, what's the next step on your side?" | Internal approval process | Get names, dates, and follow-up cadence |

**Phase 4 deliverable:** A clear commercial understanding. Consultant **sends a draft SOW within 5 working days** referencing all four phases' outputs.

## 4. Red Flags — When To Be Cautious or Walk Away

> These are NOT automatic disqualifiers — they're patterns that warrant a longer conversation or a more cautious SOW (e.g., higher deposit, scope-cap, or pilot-first engagement).

| # | Red flag | Why it matters | Recommended response |
|---|----------|----------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **No clear decision-maker** | The committee can't agree, or the "decision-maker" isn't really a decision-maker (e.g., the HR Head doesn't have budget authority) | Ask "who has the pen to sign the cheque?" If unclear after 2 meetings, walk away |
| 2 | **"Just give us a quote" with no discovery** | They want pricing without context; will compare on cost alone | Refuse to quote without Phase 1+2. Quote a discovery fee instead |
| 3 | **"We want to migrate 10 years of data"** | Scope creep; data archaeology is expensive | Cap historical migration at 3 years; archive older data in a separate report |
| 4 | **"We have 5 custom integrations"** | Each integration is a work package; this could be a 6-month project | Quote integrations separately as a fixed-fee work package; flag the 5-integration cost explicitly |
| 5 | **"We don't have time for a UAT phase"** | Without UAT, go-live is a gamble | Refuse to skip UAT. Cut scope instead — Phase 1 = core only, Phase 2 = nice-to-haves later |
| 6 | **"The Chairman will decide on price alone"** | Tells us they don't value the consulting engagement; expect monthly negotiation | Build a value-based pitch; if no traction in 2 meetings, walk away |
| 7 | **"We had a bad experience with [Darwinbox / Keka / SAP]"** | We inherit the previous vendor's failure; expectations are coloured | Acknowledge, differentiate; offer a 30-day pilot to prove the system before signing the full SOW |
| 8 | **"We want to keep our existing payroll software"** | Limits the value we can deliver; we're reduced to an HR system without payroll | Push back: payroll is 60% of the value. Counter-offer: parallel run for 3 months |
| 9 | **"No, we don't have biometric"** (and > 50 employees) | We can still deploy, but attendance will be manual for now | Recommend biometric deployment as Phase 0.1; flag attendance accuracy risk in SOW |
| 10 | **"The IT team will support but won't have time"** | Indicates IT capacity gap; we become de-facto IT support | Build managed-service line item; or refuse the engagement if we can't sustain it |

**Walk-away trigger:** Any 3+ of the above in a single meeting → politely decline the engagement, send a "not a fit at this time" email, and offer to revisit in 6 months.

## 5. Good Signs — Healthy Client Indicators

> These are patterns that increase the probability of a successful, profitable engagement. Use them to **prioritise** prospects when capacity is constrained.

| # | Good sign | Why it matters | What to do |
|---|-----------|----------------|------------|
| 1 | **A specific "why now" trigger** (audit finding, regulator deadline, board directive) | They have urgency; not just "exploring" | Fast-track to SOW within 5 working days |
| 2 | **A named, empowered project owner from their side** | Reduces our project risk; we have a counter-partner | Offer them a free 30-min onboarding to Chronova's project methodology |
| 3 | **They've already defined what success looks like** | Mature procurement; clearer SOW | Use their success criteria as the SOW acceptance criteria |
| 4 | **They're open to phased rollout** | Reduces risk for both sides | Propose Phase 1 = core HR + leave, Phase 2 = payroll + attendance |
| 5 | **Their pain is specific and quantified** (e.g., "20 leave disputes per quarter, average 5 days resolution time") | Quantifiable = we can target and measure improvement | Use the numbers in the SOW as "before / after" targets |
| 6 | **They ask about support, training, and ongoing improvement** | Signals long-term thinking, not just "buy and forget" | Walk through our support SLAs; offer AMC |
| 7 | **The Chairman or Principal is in the room** | Active executive sponsorship; removes political blockers | Send a personal follow-up to the executive within 24 hours |
| 8 | **They have prior ERP / HRMS experience** (and not too recent) | They know what they're buying; fewer surprises | Use their past experience to design the right phasing |
| 9 | **They share their data eagerly** | Transparency accelerates the engagement | Move to Phase 2 / 3 of the next meeting immediately |
| 10 | **They introduce us to their IT team** | Pre-empts the "is this secure?" question later | Schedule a separate technical call with IT within the week |

## 6. Meeting Scoring Rubric (used by Venkat internally)

After every meeting, the consultant scores the prospect on 5 dimensions (1-5 each, total 5-25). **Score < 12 = walk away; 12-17 = nurture; > 17 = prioritise.**

| # | Dimension | 1 (Poor) | 3 (Adequate) | 5 (Excellent) |
|---|-----------|----------|--------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Urgency** | "Just exploring" | "Considering in next 6 months" | "Need to go live by [specific date] for [specific reason]" |
| 2 | **Authority** | "We'd need to check with Chairman" | "HR Head will recommend to Principal" | "Chairman is in the room and has signed off on the budget" |
| 3 | **Need** | "Looking at all options" | "Comparing 2-3 vendors" | "We've defined our requirements and you're shortlisted" |
| 4 | **Budget** | "Not allocated" | "Allocated, can flex" | "Approved budget of ₹X for FY 2026-27" |
| 5 | **Fit** | "We need 10 integrations" | "Standard HR + 2-3 integrations" | "Standard HR + payroll + attendance, fits Frappe HR well" |

**Scoring cheat sheet:**

| Total score | Action |
|---|---|
| 5-11 | Politely decline. Send a "not the right fit at this time" email. Revisit in 6 months. |
| 12-17 | Nurture. Send a 2nd-meeting proposal within 2 weeks. Offer a discovery fee engagement to qualify. |
| 18-25 | Prioritise. Send a draft SOW within 5 working days. Block 2 weeks of consultant time for Phase 0. |
